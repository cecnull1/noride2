# English (英文)

## Main Content

**Note: The enchantment names in this module are very casual in English, so please be aware if you're a native English speaker.**

Several highly creative enchantments have been added, including:
- No Dismount (Players cannot dismount)
- Elytra Boost (Provides additional thrust while flying with elytra)
- Forced (Elytra) Flight (Players cannot cancel their elytra flight at any time)
- Automatic Movement (Players move automatically)
- No Sinking (Players do not sink in water and have difficulty dismounting)
- Slippery (Extremely smooth)
- Floating (Players float in the air)
- Forced Descent (Players move very slowly when trying to ascend from water)
- Forced Ladder Climbing (Always climbing ladders at any time)
- Forced Swimming (Players cannot cancel their swimming state)
- Forced Sprinting
- Boat Movement (Boats move automatically when players are riding them)
- Boat Sliding (Boats move very smoothly like ice when players are riding them)
- Free Movement (Players are unaffected in water or lava, but if underwater or in lava, they will be forced to swim and move automatically)
...

These enchantments can be applied to any armor to take effect.

# 中文（Chinese）

## 正文

添加了几个很有创意性的附魔。
其附魔包括：
- 无法下乘（玩家无法下乘）
- 鞘翅驱动（玩家在鞘翅飞行时提供额外推力）
- 强制（鞘翅）飞行（玩家任何时候都无法取消其鞘翅飞行）
- 自动移动（玩家自动移动）
- 不下沉（玩家在水中不下沉，并且难以下乘）
- 脚滑（极其的光滑）
- 飘浮（玩家飘浮在空中）
- 只能下沉（玩家试图从水中上升会很慢）
- 强制爬梯（任何时候都在爬梯）
- 强制游泳（玩家无法取消其状态）
- 强制疾跑
- 船移（玩家坐船，船会自动移动）
- 船滑（玩家坐船移动，船会像冰一样很滑）
- 自由穿行（玩在水或熔岩中不会受到影响，但如果玩家在水或熔岩下会强制性游泳并自动移动）
……

这些附魔均可以附魔在任何盔甲上使其生效。
